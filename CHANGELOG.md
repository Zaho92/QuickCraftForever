# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A bar with one icon per profession, with a skill progress bar and the skill number on it.
- Click a profession icon to open its window; click it again to close it.
- Hover a profession icon and its favorite recipes fan out. Click a recipe to craft one, click the number
  on it to craft as many as your bags allow. The profession window stays closed.
- A star next to every learned recipe in the profession window marks it as a favorite. These are the
  game's own recipe favorites.
- Recipes you lack materials for are greyed out, and their tooltip lists what is missing.
- Horizontal or vertical layout. The recipes always fan out toward the middle of the screen.
- Two looks, WoW Forever and EllesmereUI, each with or without a frame.
- Options for scale, icon size, spacing, bar thickness, bar texture, bar color, font, font size and
  animation speed. Every number has a slider and a field to type into.
- English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.
